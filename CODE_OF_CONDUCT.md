# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and technically honest.
- Assume good intent while addressing the actual impact of words and actions.
- Give and accept specific feedback without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and responsible disclosure boundaries.
- Focus disagreements on designs, evidence, and trade-offs.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, sexualized attention, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's private vulnerability-reporting channel. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is not acceptable.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
